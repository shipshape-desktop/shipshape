# Shipshape

A Mac app for getting files ready: converting, shrinking, editing, cleaning
and renaming photos, videos, audio, PDFs and Office files. It all runs on
your own Mac; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Tools: Dashboard, Autopilot (pick a goal and one run does everything it needs),
Web Pack, Convert, Optimize, Photo Editor, Video Editor, Subtitles, Archive,
Clean, Rename, Find Duplicates, Split & Merge, Inspector, Code Editor,
Favicons, History and Settings.

## Download

Get **Shipshape-1.0.1.dmg** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
It needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 11 or later.

## Opening it the first time

1. Open the .dmg and drag Shipshape onto the Applications folder beside it.
2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Shipshape.

The first time, macOS stops it: Shipshape isn't from the App Store, and it
isn't signed with an Apple developer account. To open it anyway, once:

- **macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later:** click Done. Open System Settings ›
  Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Shipshape was blocked", and click Open
  Anyway. Then open Shipshape again and click Open Anyway.
- **macOS 14 or earlier:** right-click Shipshape (or Control-click it),
  choose Open, then click Open.

After that it opens with a double-click, like any app.

Word to PDF in Convert needs LibreOffice, which is free: Shipshape shows where
to get it.
